# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Treat others with
kindness, assume good faith, and focus criticism on ideas and contributions.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, threats, personal attacks,
discrimination, sexual language or imagery, and publishing private information
without permission. Maintainers may remove comments, contributions, or access
that violate these standards.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled
promptly and as confidentially as practical.
